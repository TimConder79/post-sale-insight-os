# Five Seeds

These are stable strategic lenses, not mandatory categories.

## 1. Visibility
Make existing-customer revenue visible. New-logo revenue has stages, probabilities, inspection, and forecasts; existing-customer revenue deserves comparable visibility.

## 2. Progression
Activity is not progress. Meetings, QBRs, emails, product usage, and CSM activity are not the goal. The question is whether the customer meaningfully moved forward.

## 3. Predictability
Retention and growth become more predictable when we manage what precedes them. A renewal schedule is not a forecast. Forecasting requires evidence about specific revenue opportunities.

## 4. Execution
Design the work intended to create customer progress. Know what needs to happen next, whether it happened, and act when it does not. Completing a play is not proof of progression.

## 5. Economics
Fund the work required to move customers forward. ARR per CSM describes value, not work. Capacity starts with desired progression and the work required to support it.

## Umbrella
**The Post-Sale is the Growth Pipeline.**
