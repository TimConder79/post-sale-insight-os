# Product Definition

Post-Sale Insight OS is a personal operating system for turning external conversations into a connected body of thinking.

It helps the user find relevant conversations, preserve the source, contribute a point of view, capture what was learned, connect insights, deepen relationships, identify content worth creating, and decide what to do next.

## Core questions
- What am I learning?
- Who should I follow up with?
- What should I create next?

## Core workflow
**Today → Post Workspace → Insight → Connections → Relationship → Weekly Review → Content / Next Actions**

## Primary user
Single-user v1. The initial workflow is optimized around the product creator's market research, LinkedIn conversations, relationship development, and thought-leadership system.

## Product character
The application should feel like a thinking and execution companion, not a database the user has to maintain.
