# UX Principles

## Primary navigation
Today · Weekly Review · Insights · Relationships · Content Ideas · Searches · Settings

Within Insights: Library · Connections · Themes (Themes may remain a filter/cluster view if a separate screen adds little value).

## Experience principles
- Easy first, powerful second.
- Preserve the user's language and source material.
- Make the next useful action obvious.
- Use progressive disclosure rather than dense forms.
- Initials are sufficient; photos are optional.
- Map layout should mostly manage itself; manual dragging is available when spatial thinking helps.
- Provide a list alternative to the graph.
- AI suggestions show rationale and require approval when they change durable knowledge.
- Avoid dashboard theater: no vanity metrics, streaks, or quotas.
