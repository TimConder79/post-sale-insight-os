# Content and Authority Strategy Reference

## Operating thesis
The job is not to invent more ideas. It is to plant the same distinctive ideas consistently until the market associates the user with them.

## Content engine
**Conversation → Insight → Seed → Content → Distribution → Conversation**

A conversation should rarely end with the conversation. Capture the learning, determine the relevant seed, turn worthwhile learning into useful content, distribute it where the audience already is, and bring what is learned back into the next conversation.

## Daily discipline
**Find → Plant → Listen → Capture**

- Find conversations where there is a distinctive/relevant contribution to make.
- Plant a seed in their conversation without forcing a framework pitch.
- Listen for language, assumptions, replies, agreement, disagreement, and missing questions.
- Capture only worthwhile learning.

## Weekly discipline
**Review → Connect → Select → Create → Extend → Follow up**

The product should recommend fewer things than it finds. If a seed is underrepresented, recommend more discovery rather than manufacturing content.
